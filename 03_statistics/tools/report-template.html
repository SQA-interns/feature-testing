How to read this report
  • Pick the runs. The bar at the top shows or hides runs; every table and chart is redrawn. The × in a column header hides that run too, and any panel folds when you click its title.
  • Arrows. ↓ lower is better, ↑ higher is better, = should equal the target shown, i context only.
  • Colours in the tables. green is the best value of a row, red is the worst (or a failed gate item). Rows where all runs are equal have no colour.
  • Waiting is excluded. Agent time is wall time minus every wait for a human. A wait over one hour also expires the prompt cache; the rebuild that follows is a cost of the wait and is removed from the cost shown.
  • Same numbers for every run. They come from the session transcript where one exists, and from run-log.json otherwise. Under each attribute is what we are trying to achieve.
  • Marks after a value. † estimated, ‡ taken from a run summary or another file, * note; hover for details. “–” means the run did not record it.

Quality gate

Every run of a setup must reach the same quality; what differs is what it cost. A run below the gate delivered something else and is not comparable: its cost is shown hollow in the charts and left out of "Cost to reach the gate". Test counts are not part of the gate, they are a cost driver. A criterion marked “?” could not be assessed (the run has no such component, or an older log lacks the figure) and does not fail the gate. Mutation scopes of runs before template 1.3 differ, so their scores are only indicative.

Trust in the measurement

Every number in this report is calculated from the session transcript, the sub-agent transcripts and git, and from the run-log written by the clock tool; nothing is typed in by hand. These rows show how complete that evidence was.
